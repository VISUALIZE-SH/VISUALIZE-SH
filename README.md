<p align="center"><strong>Structural heart intelligence</strong> · <a href="https://visualize-sh.com/">visualize-sh.com</a></p>

VISUALIZE·SH is a free, source-linked map of structural heart and heart-failure
therapy: the conditions, the devices, drugs, digital therapies and procedures that
treat them, the companies behind them, and the clinical trials that test them. It
follows what changes each week and shows how each development fits the wider
landscape, with every fact traceable to its source.

> **Not medical advice.** VISUALIZE·SH is for education and information. It is not
> provided or endorsed by the organizations it lists, and it may be incomplete or
> out of date. Regulatory status, trial results and ownership change often; check
> primary sources (regulator labeling and decisions, trial registries, peer-reviewed
> publications) before relying on anything here.

## What it covers

- **Conditions:** valvular disease (aortic, mitral, tricuspid and pulmonary valves,
  paravalvular leak), left atrial appendage and atrial fibrillation, septal and
  congenital defects (PFO, ASD, VSD, PDA), heart failure (reduced and preserved
  ejection fraction), cardiomyopathies (hypertrophic, transthyretin amyloid,
  genetic dilated), coronary microvascular dysfunction and related areas.
- **Therapies:** implantable and transcatheter devices, pharmaceuticals, digital
  therapies and monitoring, and procedures, including early-stage programs, each
  with its regulatory status by jurisdiction where known.
- **Companies and trials:** developers from established manufacturers to early-stage
  start-ups, and the registered trials that evaluate their products.
- **News:** a curated, source-linked feed of regulatory decisions, trial
  registrations and results, publications and company announcements.

## How to use it

| Workspace | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **News** | Read what changed, filter by condition, product or date, and open the products and evidence each story touches. |
| **Atlas** | Explore the landscape as a graph or timeline: how conditions, therapies, companies and trials connect, and how product generations evolve. |
| **Data** | Browse specification tables by device or drug class, compare two to four exact product versions side by side, and export rows with their sources. |

Also on the site:

- **News Insights:** periodic analytical editions that explain consequential changes
  and the evidence that would change the reading next.
- **Editorials:** longer, author-written articles on device design and history.
- **Evidence panels:** every product shows its clinical data, publications,
  regulatory documents and patents (only where a public product link exists);
  **Export References** downloads the list in APA style.
- **Newsletter:** a periodic digest of approved news items, by opt-in signup.

## Where the information comes from

- Every fact links to an eligible documentary source: regulator records, trial
  registries, peer-reviewed literature, and companies' own announcements (always
  attributed to the company). Podcasts, interviews and recordings are never used as
  evidence.
- Research is done separately and reviewed by the editor before anything is
  published here. New records first appear as **drafts** (marked with a dashed
  outline and a "Draft" label) until curator review is complete.
- Unknown values are shown as gaps rather than guessed, and facts are never carried
  across product generations, patient groups, countries or indications without
  evidence.
- Attention scores (larger labels in Atlas) reflect recent news coverage, not
  clinical importance.

## Privacy

The site uses no analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies; it remembers
only your color theme in your browser. Newsletter signup happens on a separately
hosted consent form, and subscriber addresses never enter this repository. See the
[privacy notice](https://visualize-sh.com/privacy.html).
