# Authored Editorial readers

`index.yaml` connects an original reader HTML file to its explicit Editorial record in `data/news.yaml`. Each reader includes its citations and original vector diagrams. Reader content lives here; author-review artifacts remain outside public outputs.

`render-editorials.ts` copies only reviewed articles into production build output. Content approval, publication, and delivery remain separate. The October 16, 2026 Part 1 reader derives from its v4-based edition. Parts 2–4 use the current v5 editions and carry a November 3, 2026 release date. Their reader editions omit drafting notes and revision highlighting while preserving substantive text, citations, technical notes, and diagrams. Interpretation and perspective labels remain explicit. The series contents link all four parts.
