# Authored Editorial readers

`index.yaml` connects an original reader HTML file to its explicit Editorial record in `data/news.yaml`. The HTML includes the article's citations and original vector diagrams. It contains no licensed full text or third-party figures. Reader content is maintained here; generation/review evidence stays in ignored local artifacts.

`render-editorials.ts` copies only reviewed articles into production build output. The local News Insights review site explicitly includes drafts and rewrites their News links to local routes. Approval of content, publication, and delivery remain separate. The October 16, 2026 LAAO Part 1 reader derives from Part 1 v4; the separate Part 2 author revision also derives from v4. Neither uses Part 2 v5. The original v4/v5 sources remain unchanged in the local article archive.
