# LAAO / LAAC research source coverage

Review note for the local LAAO/LAAC catalog pass. These are the public source classes screened, with examples; this is not an exhaustive search of all public internet sources or a claim that every device has equivalent evidence.

| Source class | Representative sources | Role and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FDA PMA records, SSEDs, supplements, and post-approval studies | [Original WATCHMAN PMA P130013](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P130013), [Original WATCHMAN SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf13/P130013B.pdf), [Amulet SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf20/P200049B.pdf), [Original WATCHMAN PAS](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma_pas.cfm?c_id=3356&t_id=512509), [WATCHMAN FLX real-world PAS](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma_pas.cfm?c_id=6406&t_id=683187) | Primary regulatory evidence for authorization, labeled indications, device description, pivotal cohorts, and post-market follow-up. A decision or supplement is generation- and indication-specific; it does not establish comparative effectiveness or current commercial availability by itself. FDA device figures are cited to SSEDs and source locators, including [WATCHMAN FLX (P130013/S035)](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf13/P130013S035B.pdf), [WATCHMAN FLX Pro (P130013/S074)](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf13/P130013S074B.pdf), and [Amulet (P200049)](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf20/P200049B.pdf). |
| ClinicalTrials.gov | [Amulet IDE, NCT02879448](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02879448), [CLOSURE-AF, NCT03463317](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03463317) | Primary trial registry for design, enrollment, status, and posted results. Registry entries can be incomplete or updated after publication; published reports take precedence for analyzed outcomes. |
| Peer-reviewed trials and primary studies | [Amulet IDE, Circulation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34459659/), [WATCHMAN FLX early study, JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jcin.2020.06.056), [CHAMPION-AF, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2517213), [CLOSURE-AF, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2513310) | Primary clinical evidence. Trial population, comparator, endpoint, follow-up, and device generation bound the result; a study of a device class or a mixed-device surgical intervention is not assigned to an individual product unless the source supports that link. PubMed is used to locate and identify papers, not as a substitute for the paper. |
| ACC news, journal scans, and NCDR | [ACC Latest in Cardiology](https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology#sort=%40commonsortdate%20descending), [ACC NCDR LAAO Registry](https://cvquality.acc.org/NCDR-Home/registries/hospital-registries/laao-registry) | ACC coverage helps surface trial, registry, policy, and practice developments; the registry provides real-world procedural and quality context. ACC summaries are secondary; registry analyses are observational and can be affected by case selection, coding, and follow-up limits. Dated articles and original analyses are cited at item level when used. |
| Northwestern Medicine physician news | [Breakthroughs for Physicians](https://breakthroughsforphysicians.nm.org/) | Academic health-system research and clinical coverage discovery source. Site coverage may be selective or promotional; device-specific claims need dated article-level citations and corroboration from primary evidence. |
| ESC and meeting coverage | [ESC Congress](https://www.escardio.org/Congresses-Events/ESC-Congress), [ESC abstracts](https://esc365.escardio.org/) | Meeting programs and abstracts can surface late-breaking results before full publication. Abstract data may be preliminary, abbreviated, or not peer-reviewed; it is labeled accordingly and not treated as a full paper. |
| Cardiovascular trade reporting | [TCTMD](https://www.tctmd.com/) | Useful for dated meeting and trial reporting, interviews, and practice context. It is secondary coverage; quantitative claims are checked against a paper, registry, regulatory record, or meeting abstract where available. |
| Manufacturer materials and releases | [Abbott Amulet 360 announcement](https://abbott.mediaroom.com/2026-08-27-Abbotts-next-generation-Amulet-360-TM-device-receives-CE-Mark-to-treat-people-with-atrial-fibrillation-at-risk-of-stroke), [Boston Scientific CHAMPION-AF results](https://www.bostonscientific.com/en-EU/products/laac-system/champion-af-clinical-trial/trial-results.html) | Useful for product descriptions, company announcements, and sponsor-reported milestones. These sources have commercial interests; claims about safety, efficacy, status, and availability are bounded to the release and checked against independent or regulatory records when possible. |

## Where the evidence is recorded

- Device versions, historical devices, and their source locators: [`data/intelligence/catalog/laao-spectrum.yaml`](../data/intelligence/catalog/laao-spectrum.yaml) and [`data/intelligence/catalog/occluders.yaml`](../data/intelligence/catalog/occluders.yaml).
- FDA original WATCHMAN, Amulet IDE, trial, registry, and ACC references: [`data/intelligence/catalog/laao-fda-evidence.yaml`](../data/intelligence/catalog/laao-fda-evidence.yaml).
- FDA-derived device figures and citations: [`data/intelligence/catalog/figures.yaml`](../data/intelligence/catalog/figures.yaml), with local image assets under [`public/evidence/fda/`](../public/evidence/fda/). The WATCHMAN FLX, FLX Pro, and Amulet figures cite their respective FDA SSEDs.
- Dated news items and their article-level citations: [`data/news.yaml`](../data/news.yaml), including the Amulet 360 announcement, CHAMPION-AF, CLOSURE-AF, WATCHMAN post-ablation labeling, and ACC LAAO Registry coverage.

Claims and images in the catalog should retain their item-level source references and locators. Broad discovery pages are included as search entry points, not as citations for individual facts.
