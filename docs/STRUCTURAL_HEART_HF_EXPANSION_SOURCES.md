# Structural heart and HF expansion: source ledger

Review ledger for the six added Data categories, as of 2026-09-28. FDA records support US authorization and the labeled indication; registries support trial identity, design, and status; original trial papers support analyzed outcomes. Evidence for one device or trial is not generalized to a whole treatment class.

| Category | Primary FDA, registry, and clinical sources | Status and source-specific caveats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paravalvular leak closure** | [Occlutech PLD multicenter registry, midterm results (PMID 31422926)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31422926/); [PLD long-term multicenter outcomes (PMCID PMC8999580)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8999580/); [KISS multicenter registry (PMID 38156599)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38156599/); [international prospective PVL registry (PMID 36256693)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36256693/). | The PLD is a dedicated device with European use; the reviewed FDA databases did not identify US authorization for this product. KISS included Amplatzer Vascular Plug III (AVP III) in 140 of 171 transcatheter cases, but its pooled closure results are not AVP III-specific. AVP III use for PVL closure is generally off-label in the US; no FDA authorization specifically for that use was identified. These registries include multiple device types and cannot establish comparative performance for either plug. |
| **Mitral chordal reconstruction** | [FDA De Novo DEN230069, Mi-CHORD](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN230069.pdf); [FDA De Novo decision record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/denovo.cfm?id=DEN230069); [ReChord registry, NCT02803957](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02803957); [NeoChord DS1000 Summary of Safety and Clinical Performance](https://www.neochord.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/RP-602000-001_DS1000_SSCP_REV_C_Final.pdf). | Mi-CHORD received De Novo classification on 2024-06-11; its FDA-reviewed clinical dataset was a prospective, single-center 12-patient cohort, not a large pivotal randomized trial. ReChord evaluates NeoChord DS1000 against open repair; the registry was active, not recruiting, at its 2026-04-08 update and had no results posted. The two systems are separate products with different regulatory and evidence status. |
| **Heterotopic caval valves** | [TRICAVAL registry, NCT02387697](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02387697); [TRICAVAL trial paper (PMID 31929100)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31929100/); [TRICUS EURO six-month paper (PMID 35583363)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35583363/); [TRICUS EURO one-year report](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jcin.2023.10.043); [TRICENTO multicenter registry (PMID 35236096)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35236096/); [US TRICAV IDE, NCT06137807](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06137807). | **TRICAVAL is halted/terminated**: the SAPIEN XT IVC study stopped after valve embolization/dislodgement events, including surgical retrieval. This status applies to that trial and technique; it does not establish that TricValve, TRICENTO, all bicaval systems, or all SAPIEN XT use were halted. TRICUS EURO is a separate, single-arm European study of TricValve. TRICENTO has a small retrospective 21-patient registry, reported in European practice; no FDA authorization was identified. |
| **Left ventricular restoration** | [ALIVE trial registry, NCT02931240](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02931240); [ALIVE primary report, JACC: Heart Failure](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jchf.2024.09.023); [earlier Revivent TC feasibility report (PMCID PMC9725051)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9725051/); [CORCINCH-HF registry, NCT04331769](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04331769); [AccuCinch two-year follow-up paper, Structural Heart (DOI 10.1016/j.shj.2025.100694)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.shj.2025.100694). | ALIVE studied Revivent TC in a selected ischemic scar population. At one year, LV dimensions fell, but the hierarchical clinical-effectiveness endpoint did not significantly differ (win ratio 1.13; P=0.32). AccuCinch is a separate investigational system: the CORCINCH-HF registry's June 2026 update reports the pivotal US study suspended; this trial status does not establish product retirement. FDA Breakthrough Device Designation is not marketing authorization. No US marketing authorization was identified for either system. |
| **Epicardial restraint** | [PEERLESS-HF registry, NCT00382863](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00382863); [PEERLESS-HF randomized trial report (PMID 22633302)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22633302/); [Acorn five-year randomized-trial follow-up (PMCID PMC3790142)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3790142/). | **PEERLESS-HF is halted**: enrollment was suspended after interim futility for the peak-VO₂ endpoint. That is a trial stop, not proof that every epicardial restraint device was retired. CorCap's status as a historical/retired product is reported in the peer-reviewed review [“Organ-saving surgical alternatives to treatment of heart failure,” Cardiovascular Diagnosis and Therapy (2021), DOI 10.21037/cdt-20-285](https://cdt.amegroups.org/article/view/48028/html); treat that retirement claim as secondary-source evidence. FDA did not approve the CorCap PMA. The Acorn five-year follow-up concerns the 107-person no-MVR stratum of the original randomized trial; it is distinct from the 50-person confirmatory study, NCT00630266. |
| **Durable mechanical circulatory support** | [FDA HeartMate II PMA P060040](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P060040); [FDA HeartMate 3 PMA P160054 SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf16/P160054B.pdf); [MOMENTUM 3 registry, NCT02224755](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02224755); [MOMENTUM 3 two-year randomized report (PMID 28546350)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28546350/); [five-year MOMENTUM 3 follow-up (PMID 36074476)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36074476/); [FDA post-approval study](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfPMA/pma_pas.cfm?c_id=5011&t_id=642305). | HeartMate II and HeartMate 3 have FDA PMA authorization for their respective labeled indications; current commercial availability may differ from authorization status. The 2026 FDA Class II [recall record Z-2518-2026](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfres/res.cfm?id=220518) is limited to specified 11V lithium-ion backup batteries, identified by serial number, in affected HeartMate 3 LVAS kits model 106524US. It does not designate all 106524US kits or all implanted HM3 pumps as recalled. The separate 2024 [FDA Class I implant-kit correction](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-recalls-and-early-alerts/abbott-recalls-heartmate-3-left-ventricular-assist-system-lvas-implant-kit-risk-blood-leakage-or-air) was likewise described as a correction, not product removal. |
