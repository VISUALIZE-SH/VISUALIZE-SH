# LAAO/LAAC and septal/congenital occluder catalog audit

> Domain review notes from before final integration. See the [consolidated audit](../DATABASE_AUDIT_2026-10-03.md) for the applied corrections, subsequent primary-source checks, and remaining gaps. Candidate findings are archived in [2026-10-03-laa-findings.json](2026-10-03-laa-findings.json).


**Audit date:** 2026-10-03. **Scope:** all 32 version rows and all attached specification blocks in `laao-spectrum.yaml`, `occluders.yaml`, and `laao-fda-evidence.yaml`; graph therapies/companies were read-only. Catalog records remain draft. Existing owner-approved early FLX, SeaLA ownership, suspended LAMINAR, and Amulet 8-size corrections were preserved.

## Catalog versions inspected

Every version below was inspected. Listed attributes are the sourced specification claims currently present in the catalog; omission means the fragment has no claim for that attribute, not that the attribute is necessarily undisclosed. Specifications remain generation-specific.

Later availability is unresolved. [ev3 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318310/000110465906052498/a06-15615_110q.htm); [Delaware Supreme Court case](https://law.justia.com/cases/delaware/supreme-court/2007/98990.html).
- Added the six missing septal/congenital `graphEntityId` links in `occluders.yaml`, including GORE CARDIOFORM ASD, and linked that therapy ID into the existing CARDIOFORM family.
- Added a sourced therapy/company proposal for `dev-gore-cardioform-asd` to the JSON proposal file. FDA identifies a 2019-05-28 PMA supplement; W. L. Gore current product material states the US ostium secundum ASD indication. Exact configuration-specific implant material details remain unfilled because the accessible product page pointed to an IFU that could not be retrieved in this audit.
- Added sourced graph-company proposals for all 20 LAA therapy nodes with missing `company` mappings. These distinguish historical developer from acquired owner where the source supports it; they do not assert present availability.
- Removed or replaced all 12 owned spec claims defaulted to the 2018 CI Today spectrum review: Ultraseal now uses its primary multicenter paper; LARIAT access uses its original clinical report (which identifies SentreHEART and combined pericardial/transseptal access; the Cardioblate IDE registry NCT00841529 was excluded from LARIAT evidence); Sideris retains only the bioabsorbable patch/adhesive detail supported by its original study abstract; unsupported Sideris material/sheath values and all Prolipsis and SeaLA spec claims were dropped pending primary evidence. No spec values still depend on that review.
- Kept generation boundaries explicit: original WATCHMAN vs 2015 FLX and redesigned FLX; original Occlutech vs Plus; Ultraseal vs Ultraseal II; historic Lefort vs MemoLefort; AtriClip vs Ecliptis; Amplatzer Cardiac Plug vs Amulet/Amulet 360. No evidence or composition was transferred between versions.
- Septal/congenital family manufacturers are already mapped for Abbott Amplatzer PFO/ASD/PDA/VSD, W. L. Gore CARDIOFORM, Lifetech LAmbre, and Edwards Ecliptis. The ASD add-on, Piccolo supplement, and device-specific original approval sources retain their own dates, indications, and limitations.

## Material and specification scope

Primary sources support reported materials for many versions, including both Amulet generations, Ultraseal I/II, LACbes, Lefort, and FDA-authorized septal devices. The original WATCHMAN FLX 2015 iteration has only one design claim; Amulet 360 and Cardioblate are partly described; SeaLA and Prolipsis currently have no catalog spec claims; Sierra ligature, Sideris patch polymer and sheath French size, and Laminar lock material remain unverified. The spec blocks omit attributes where the exact version-specific primary source did not support a value.

## Unresolved evidence gaps

- **Current market status / geography:** most legacy products are covered by old trial reports, a past CE statement, or discontinued-program disclosures; these do not establish present sale or authorization country by country. WaveCrest’s 2015 acquisition release states 2013 CE Mark and no US commercial distribution then, but present distribution remains unknown: [Biosense Webster acquisition announcement](https://www.biosensewebster.com/documents/TaylorPressRelease.pdf?Cache=20%2F11%2F2015+12%3A16%3A45).
- **PLAATO:** its current availability following ev3’s 2005 discontinuation is unresolved.
- **Prolipsis:** no primary manufacturer, regulator, registry, or original clinical paper establishes its design lineage or maker. The grouped family is for discovery only; its company and spec fields remain unset. The existing graph subtype, “second-generation” regulatory detail, and bioabsorbable/immediate-release mechanism lack Prolipsis-specific primary support and should be withdrawn unless a direct primary source is obtained. The secondary consensus review is only a discovery lead.
- **SeaLA:** the 2024 primary multicenter paper confirms clinical use but does not disclose composition/design details in its abstract; historical geometry/material/hook values from the 2018 review were removed. The LAA-occluder patent record identifies Hangzhou Nuomao Medtech Co., Ltd. as original assignee and records a 2022 change of name to Hangzhou Dinova EP Technology Co., Ltd.; this supports using the current Dinova name with Nuomao as the former name of the same patent assignee. ClinicalTrials.gov separately lists Dinova as trial sponsor, which alone does not establish manufacturer. Primary NMPA decision and current availability were not confirmed. [US10792045B2 patent/assignment record](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10792045B2/en); [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05911685](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05911685).
- **LAMINAR current ownership:** J&J confirmed its 2023 acquisition and ClinicalTrials.gov shows the pivotal study suspended, but a later asset transfer reported in secondary press lacked a primary transaction source during this audit. [J&J acquisition](https://www.investor.jnj.com/investor-news/news-details/2023/Johnson--Johnson-MedTech-Acquires-Laminar-Inc/default.aspx); [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06168942](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06168942).
- **LACBES authorization:** primary studies establish the manufacturer and clinical use; an NMPA approval decision record was not located. US/EU authorization is also unresolved.
- **CARDIOFORM ASD and Piccolo:** the relevant PMA supplement records identify authorization but no standalone SSED/IFU was public in the sources reviewed. Keep study eligibility, approval-order text, and verbatim labeling clearly separated.
- **LARIAT and surgical clips:** tool clearance or a clip’s concomitant-surgery clearance does not by itself establish an FDA-approved LAA stroke-prevention indication.
- **Sideris patch:** the original abstract verifies bioabsorbable patch and two-stage adhesive, but not the patch polymer or 13 Fr value; both review-derived details were removed.
