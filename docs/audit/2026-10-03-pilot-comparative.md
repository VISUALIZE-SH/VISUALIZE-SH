## Findings and proposals

The SAPIEN 3 Ultra RESILIA figure record points to FDA S085 PDF page 100, Figure 1, model 9750TFX. The FDA labels that figure as standard SAPIEN 3 Ultra and describes ThermaFix-treated leaflets. The figure can serve as a platform reference, but it does not depict the RESILIA valve; the assertion that RESILIA changes only tissue treatment is not supported. FDA S162 and Edwards’ product material distinguish RESILIA tissue and describe design features of the named RESILIA product. Proposed wording qualifies the drawing as a platform reference and removes the unsupported equivalence claim. Sources: [FDA S085 IFU](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf14/P140031S085D.pdf), [FDA S162 labeling](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf14/P140031S162C.pdf), [Edwards SAPIEN 3 Ultra RESILIA product page](https://www.edwards.com/healthcare-professionals/products-services/transcatheter-heart/transcatheter-sapien-3-ultra-resilia).

The TriClip G4 FDA SSED’s PDF page 2, Figure 1 is explicitly the system plus stabilizer accessory; its SSED text describes the steerable guide catheter, delivery system, and accessories. The separately cited FDA labeling page 4, Figure 1 is the clip-implant positions image. This supports keeping system and implant images distinct. Only the SSED system locator was directly re-opened in this pass. Source: [FDA TriClip G4 SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf23/P230007B.pdf).

The three publication-figure records reviewed are compatible with CC BY reuse: the LAmbre paper states the Creative Commons Attribution License; both Frontiers articles state CC BY 4.0. Reducer Figure 1 is the expanded reducer image. Corvia Figure 4A is the IASD device image; a separate Figure 3 in that same article expressly identifies Elsevier-permission material, so that caveat must not be attached to Figure 4A. Captions should identify author(s), article, exact panel, link the article and license, and disclose cropping. CC BY 4.0 requires appropriate attribution, a license link, and disclosure of changes. Sources: [LAmbre article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9352115/), [Reducer article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cardiovascular-medicine/articles/10.3389/fcvm.2016.00042/full), [Corvia article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cardiovascular-medicine/articles/10.3389/fcvm.2019.00143/full), [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

No product version was assigned to comparative measurements beyond the cited configurations; annulus recommendations and delivery-catheter profiles are labeled specifications, not clinical outcomes. No numeric corrections are proposed. Existing explicit missing-outcome fields remain missing; registry status or absent follow-up does not establish a zero event rate.

Primary-source numeric checks include: ACORN enrolled 107 in its no-MVR stratum (57 device/50 control); its mean five-year LVEDV difference was −28.9 mL, P=.029. EXCLUDE observed 67/70 intraoperative exclusions (95.7%) and 60/61 among those imaged at three months (98.4%; 65/70 available for assessment). In SWISS-APERO, the 96.4%/97.3% follow-up rates and 80/76 patients with three-year clinical endpoint follow-up are different source statements; the record's 80/76 endpoint-assessable counts are correct and must not be silently replaced by the overall follow-up rates.

The reported 95% CI for the VITAL-HF outcome was checked against the primary paper. The paper's table rounds the least-squares difference interval to 0.2–1.3, while the abstract/text gives 0.21–1.34; the dataset stores the more precise reported values. For SWISS-APERO the source publication date is 30 June 2025 online and 8 July 2025 issue; the dataset's July 8 date is issue publication rather than online-first date.

### Registry and material source gaps

ReChord's official ClinicalTrials.gov record reports NCT02803957, sponsor NeoChord, 585 estimated enrollment, active/not recruiting, actual primary completion January 2021, estimated study completion January 2027, record update April 8, 2026, and no results posted. This is a study plan/status record, not outcome evidence. For TRICAVAL, the original study publication directly says early stop was due to valve dislocations/safety; registry-specific enrollment/status details still need official record refresh. For CORCINCH-HF, suspension must not be represented as market withdrawal or device discontinuation; official registry state needs refresh before date-specific lifecycle claims.

Abbott primary labeling resolves the Navitor NaviSeal wording: the current US Navitor IFU calls both cuff portions polyethylene; Abbott's Navitor product-materials IFU gives the cuff fabric as polyethylene and specifies UHMWPE in its substance table (PDF p.13, Product Materials table). This is a generic-family vs polymer-grade distinction, not a material conflict. Recommended parent-side normalization: `UHMWPE (polyethylene) fabric, inner and outer NaviSeal cuff`; cite the product-materials IFU p.13 plus the current US IFU's NaviSeal description. Do not change any shared YAML in this subtask.
