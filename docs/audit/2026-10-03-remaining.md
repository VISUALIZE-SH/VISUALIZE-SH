## Findings by record

- **Revivent TC / BioVentrix:** The primary ALIVE report is the appropriate source for its trial outcome; it reports a 12-month hierarchical efficacy outcome that was not significantly different from control (win ratio 1.13; P=.32). ClinicalTrials.gov NCT01568138 is a primary trial record naming BioVentrix as information provider and linking its sponsor site. Neither source establishes current market availability, regional authorization, exact materials, or continuing corporate ownership. [ALIVE report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39797846/); [NCT01568138](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01568138).
- **CorCap / Acorn Cardiovascular:** The five-year Acorn primary paper supports the trial cohort and outcomes, and its full-text Introduction explicitly describes the device as a polyester mesh fitted around the ventricles. That supports the material/design claim at this broad level; avoid more specific weave/construction claims unless directly sourced. The catalog's statement that CorCap was completely withdrawn after FDA non-approval still depends on a review, not direct company or regulator evidence located here. Keep withdrawal timing/status qualified pending a primary source. [Acorn five-year paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3790142/).
- **HeartNet / Paracor:** The PEERLESS-HF paper and registry support investigational study status and enrollment stopping after futility; they do not establish current regulatory status, availability, corporate status, or precise construction materials. [PEERLESS-HF paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22633302/); [NCT00382863](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00382863).
- **AccuCinch / Ancora Heart:** The CORCINCH-HF registry identifies the pivotal study and the Ancora-sponsored early-feasibility registry; the two-year paper is a separate earlier-study follow-up. Keep pivotal enrollment targets separate from observed two-year results. The bounded primary set supports investigational status and company association, but not authorization, current marketing, or exact materials. [CORCINCH-HF NCT04331769](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04331769); [early feasibility NCT03533517](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03533517); [two-year report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.shj.2025.100694).
- **HeartMate II:** The FDA's original PMA identifies Abbott Medical as the current applicant while retaining the historical Thoratec HeartMate name. The April 21, 2008 approval was for bridge-to-transplantation; the FDA-approved destination-therapy expansion came later through supplement S005 (January 20, 2010). The SSED identifies titanium alloy Ti-6Al-4V, polyester grafts, and ceramic bearings for the HeartMate II generation; do not transfer these to HeartMate 3. The PMA is US regulatory evidence and does not independently establish current availability worldwide. [P060040](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P060040); [original SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf6/P060040B.pdf); [S005 SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf6/P060040S005b.pdf).
- **HeartMate 3:** FDA P160054 records the August 23, 2017 PMA decision and Abbott Medical as the applicant; FDA establishment records separately show Thoratec as the registered establishment and Abbott as owner/operator. These support the applicant/ownership distinction but do not support importing HeartMate II materials. Current labeling requires review of the PMA supplements, and the PMA remains US-specific. [P160054](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P160054); [FDA registration/listing](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfRL/rl.cfm?lid=564610&lpcd=DSQ).
- **HeartMate 3 kit battery recall:** FDA recall Z-2518-2026 is open and Class II. It targets specified 11V backup batteries in model 106524US kits, with affected serials in the agency attachment. Its text gives 24 complaints and names limited US and overseas distribution; it does not recall all pumps or every kit. The FDA instructions identify the recalled battery as lithium-ion; the claim is limited to the affected batteries in the recall. [FDA recall Z-2518-2026](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfres/res.cfm?id=220518).
- **Corcym TriMemo:** FDA 510(k) K260498 lists Corcym S.r.l., device name, and a substantial-equivalence decision dated April 14, 2026. Its summary describes a semirigid tricuspid annuloplasty ring with an electropolished Nitinol core, silicone sewing ring, knitted PET fabric, and Carbofilm turbostratic-carbon coating. This supports US clearance and materials for TriMemo itself. The summary states six sizes from 26–36 mm in 2 mm increments (PDF page 5, printed Summary page 1, device description/materials section). Corcym's own EACTS 2026 page announces the Memo platform launch and reports that first-in-human experience will be presented, but does not support the graph's specific Sept. 8 implant date or US commercial availability. [FDA K260498 summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf26/K260498.pdf); [FDA decision record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K260498); [Corcym EACTS page](https://info.corcym.com/corcym-at-eacts-2026/launch-symposium).
- **Corcym Memo 4D Curve:** FDA K261707 lists Corcym and a substantial-equivalence decision dated June 18, 2026. Its 510(k) Summary (PDF page 5, Device Description Summary) says superelastic Nitinol core, silicone elastomer, knitted polyester with Carbofilm, and PET thread with Carbofilm; it specifies ten sizes from 24–42 mm in 2 mm steps (PDF pages 5–6). The direct Corcym EACTS page confirms launch and first-in-human/robotic experience will be presented but supplies no first-use date or proof of commercial availability. [FDA K261707 summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf26/K261707.pdf); [FDA decision record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K261707); [Corcym EACTS page](https://info.corcym.com/corcym-at-eacts-2026/launch-symposium).
- **Corcym company date:** Corcym's own launch announcement says global operations launched June 1, 2021, after its acquisition/carve-out of LivaNova's heart-valve business. A 2022 start date is incorrect. [Corcym launch announcement](https://www.corcym.com/press-release-day-one).
- **AZD5462 / AstraZeneca:** AstraZeneca's pipeline lists AZD5462 for heart failure at Phase II in its July 27, 2026 snapshot. This supports current company attribution and Phase II, while the original discovery article supports AZD5462 as a selective oral allosteric RXFP1 agonist. The 2026 translational paper supports Phase I human safety/pharmacokinetic work and says a Phase II study is underway. ESC’s trial report identifies LUMINARA as Phase IIb in 375 participants: 235 with LVEF ≤35% and 140 with LVEF 41–55%, over 24 weeks. It describes the lowest-dose primary LVESVI comparison as not statistically significant (P=.054); do not say the trial demonstrated a significant primary benefit or HF clinical-outcome benefit. Phase III outcomes testing is still needed. [AZD5462 discovery paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38502782/); [2026 translational paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42137267/); [ESC LUMINARA report](https://www.escardio.org/news/press/press-releases/novel-drug-shows-promise-in-an-early-phase-trial-in-heart-failure/); [AstraZeneca pipeline](https://www.astrazeneca.com/our-therapy-areas/pipeline.html).
- **Tirzepatide / Eli Lilly:** Lilly's SUMMIT page and the ClinicalTrials.gov record identify tirzepatide, NCT04847557, a Phase III study with target enrollment 731, and Eli Lilly as sponsor. This supports trial identity and company relationship, not HFpEF labeling. The therapy's current approval scopes must be checked against regulators for each geography; do not treat guidelines or trial results as a label change. The graph's speculative direct-filling-pressure mechanism is not supported by these sources, and the Wikipedia source should be replaced. [Lilly SUMMIT page](https://trials.lilly.com/en-US/trial/282998); [NCT04847557](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04847557).

## Structural-only evidence

FDA device decisions support US regulatory status and described construction; they do not establish outcomes, first-human dates, present commercial distribution, or approval outside the United States. Trial registries and primary clinical papers support trial identity/status/outcomes but do not by themselves establish market availability. Exact material statements above are limited to the specific FDA device generation, and HeartMate II materials must not carry over to HeartMate 3.
