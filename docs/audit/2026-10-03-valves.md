## Findings and unresolved gaps

- **Identity and regulatory scope verified:** ACURATE neo2 was CE-marked but never FDA-authorized. Boston Scientific's 2025 Q3 release says it decided in Q2 2025 to discontinue worldwide sales of neo2 and Prime. Keep discontinuation attached to those commercial platforms; the source does not establish withdrawal of implants already in patients. Direct sources: [Boston Scientific Q3 2025 release](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-10-22-Boston-Scientific-announces-results-for-third-quarter-2025), [ACURATE official product page](https://www.bostonscientific.com/en-US/medical-specialties/structural-heart/acurate-neo2-for-physicians.html).
- **Trilogy:** FDA PMA P250024 decision is 2026-03-17; S003 is a later supplement dated 2026-07-09. Original SSED p.2 describes frame/leaflets; p.1 gives the US native severe AR indication. The existing spec and version correctly retain FDA-only US scope and distinguish native AR from EU AS/AR labeling. Sources: [FDA SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf25/P250024B.pdf), [FDA S003 record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?PMANumber=P250024&SupplementNumber=S003&start_search=1).
- **Melody/Harmony:** FDA confirms Melody's transition from HDE H080002 to full PMA P140017 for conduit/surgical pulmonary valve use. Melody's SSED identifies platinum-iridium stent and bovine jugular valve but does not disclose the permanent frame-fixation suture polymer (p.2); leave that material unresolved. Harmony SSED p.2 confirms nitinol, polyester graft, porcine pericardium and different suture compositions by size. The existing Harmony entry is family-level TPV22/25; preserve that qualification in any therapy material proposal. Sources: [Melody SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf14/P140017B.pdf), [Harmony SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf20/P200046B.pdf), [Harmony IFU](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf20/P200046D.pdf).
- **Generation conflation avoided:** MitraClip G4 adds NTW/XTW and independent/simultaneous grippers; G5 is a delivery handle/IFU update to the implant. TriClip G5 likewise carries the G4 implant. FDA P100009/S078 (2025-08-20) is a subsequent G5 IFU clarification, not a new implant generation or indication expansion. Sources: [G5 FDA record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?ID=P100009S078), [TriClip FDA SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf23/P230007B.pdf).
- **Mitral/tricuspid approvals:** Tendyne P240042 (2025-05-23) is for severe MAC-associated mitral valve dysfunction in patients unsuitable for surgery/TEER; SAPIEN M3 P250019 (2025-12-22) has MR and MAC-associated scopes; EVOQUE P230013 (2024-02-01) covers symptomatic severe TR despite OMT. Catalog specs identify the approved version and scope distinctly. Sources: [Tendyne FDA PMA](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P240042), [SAPIEN M3 FDA PMA](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P250019), [EVOQUE FDA PMA](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P230013).
- **Material opportunity proposals integrated into graph:** Current therapy entries now include Mi-CHORD ePTFE chordae and titanium fasteners, TricValve nitinol frames / bovine-pericardial leaflets / location-specific skirts, and TRICENTO nitinol, porcine pericardium, and PTFE. These materials were supported by the catalog evidence; no missing-material recommendation remains for these three entries. Sources: [Mi-CHORD FDA De Novo summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN230069.pdf), [TricValve technical specifications](https://productsandfeatures.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Tricvalve_Technical_Specifications_2023.pdf), [TRICENTO manufacturer patient leaflet](https://www.nvt-med.com/files/Content/Downloads/Patient%20Leaflet%20TRICENTO_Rev_00105601_072021_02.pdf).
- **Further material gaps:** Occlutech PLD publication describes a self-expanding nitinol-braided device (article, device-description section), but no current therapy material is entered. AVP III is used in observational PVL closure; its US approval for that use is not established, and its material should not be inferred from another Amplatzer plug generation. SAPIEN XT caval use is off-label/investigational in TRICAVAL and should not be conflated with its approved aortic prosthesis. NeoChord SSCP identifies the DS1000 implant, but exact permanent implant composition was not asserted here without a locator-level review. Cardioband is CE-marked (mitral then tricuspid) with no US PMA identified; technical size detail remains incomplete in the cited review. AdapTR evidence remains a single case; the textile skirt polymer remains undisclosed. Sources: [Occlutech PLD original study](https://www.occlutech.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Onorato-2019-Midterm-procedural-and-clinical-outcomes-of-percutaneous-paravalvular-leak-closure-with-the-Occlutech-PLD.pdf), [KISS PVL registry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10863827/), [TRICAVAL registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02387697), [NeoChord SSCP](https://www.neochord.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/RP-602000-001_DS1000_SSCP_Rev_B_FINAL_05Dec2024.docx.pdf), [Cardioband study](https://eurointervention.pcronline.com/article/the-cardioband-transcatheter-direct-mitral-valve-annuloplasty-system), [adapTR first-in-human report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12926008/).
- **Navitor material wording resolved:** Abbott's International IFU (ARTMT600163776 Rev. B, PDF p.13, Product Materials table) identifies NaviSeal sealing fabric as UHMWPE; the US IFU describes the cuff generically as polyethylene. These are generic polymer versus grade-level descriptions, not a material conflict. The catalog's polyethylene (UHMWPE) wording and therapy material are compatible. The cited 2022 IFU predates the FDA-added 35 mm Navitor Titan size, so it does not support that size. Sources: [Abbott International IFU](https://manuals.eifu.abbott/content/dam/av/manuals-eifu/global/IS/en/ARTMT600163776_B.PDF), [Abbott US IFU](https://manuals.eifu.abbott/content/dam/av/manuals-eifu/global/US/en/ARTEN600249797_A.pdf), [FDA S013 record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P190023S013).
- **SAPIEN XT original indication correction recommended:** Current graph/catalog summary equates the original pivotal inoperable cohort with the original PMA label. FDA's June 16, 2014 original P130009 approval instead covered severe native calcific AS in patients at high-or-greater surgical risk (STS ≥8% or predicted 30-day mortality ≥15%). The pivotal Cohort B was inoperable; later Cohort A/high-risk evidence supported expanded access. Distinguish cohort from indication and cite the original order. Sources: [FDA original P130009 order](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P130009), [FDA original SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf13/P130009B.pdf).
- **SAPIEN XT PARTNER 2A citation:** The version's PARTNER 2A generation statement is accurate: the trial used the second-generation SAPIEN XT. Its current version source reference is the original P130009 SSED, which does not verify PARTNER 2A. Cite the PARTNER II Cohort A primary paper or relevant FDA post-approval documentation. Sources: [PARTNER 2 Cohort A primary study](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1514616), [FDA P130009/S057 post-approval study](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfPMA/pma_pas.cfm?c_id=4105&t_id=594156).
- **SAPIEN 3 Ultra RESILIA sizing correction recommended:** Current spec marks RESILIA sizes/anatomy as uncertain based on S085/S141. FDA's later consolidated labeling S182D explicitly lists RESILIA model 9755RSL sizes 20, 23, 26, and 29 mm in Table 1 (PDF p.2) and says native sizing recommendations in Table 4 (PDF p.2) apply to SAPIEN 3, Ultra, and Ultra RESILIA, including 20 mm 16–19 mm, 23 mm 18–22 mm, 26 mm 21–25 mm, and 29 mm 24–28 mm TEE annuli. Table 5 gives shared failing-bioprosthesis true-ID sizing and Table 6 gives INSPIRIS RESILIA compatibility. Table 9 (PDF p.9) matches 9755RSL20/23/26/29 with Commander 9750CM20/23/26/29. That labeling only says the sheath is supplied by Edwards; it does not specify exact French size/model, so retain a gap for the RESILIA-specific sheath size unless a separate current sheath IFU is cited. Source: [FDA S182D labeling](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf14/P140031S182D.pdf).
- **SAPIEN 3 Ultra RESILIA change-set wording needs qualification:** Current catalog context says S141 “changes tissue and skirt material only.” FDA S162 SSED describes Ultra RESILIA as a SAPIEN 3 Ultra design iteration with RESILIA leaflets and repeats its platform frame and skirt construction; patient labeling likewise separates the RESILIA tissue feature. These records substantiate shared platform properties and the leaflet distinction, but not an exhaustive “only” change list or a skirt-material change. Either remove “only” and mark complete change-set as unresolved, or cite a direct design-change comparison. Sources: [FDA S162 SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf14/P140031S162B.pdf), [FDA S162 patient labeling](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf14/P140031S162C.pdf), [FDA S141 record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P140031S141).
- **Memo 4D Curve size-specific shape:** Although all sizes are an open semirigid mitral annuloplasty family and open-ring status is correct, the current family/version summary “open 3D” overgeneralizes the size range. The FDA K261707 summary says 24–32 mm sizes are flat, and only 34–42 mm are 3D/out-of-plane. Keep 3D qualified to those larger sizes. Source: [FDA K261707 summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf26/K261707.pdf), physical PDF pp.5–6 (510(k) Summary pp.1–2).
- **Lifecycle:** ACURATE neo2 discontinued worldwide commercial sales in 2025, no FDA authorization; CorCap is historical/retired per secondary literature and was not an FDA-approved product; TRICAVAL SAPIEN XT IVC study was terminated for safety concerns but that does not mean the underlying SAPIEN XT valve was withdrawn. Other investigational status must not be presented as product withdrawal. Sources: [Boston Scientific Q3 2025 release](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-10-22-Boston-Scientific-announces-results-for-third-quarter-2025), [TRICAVAL registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02387697), [CorCap five-year study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3790142/).

No catalog company recommendation was warranted: the manufacturer/product pairings inspected agree with FDA or manufacturer records. `co-nvt` uses the name on its own product leaflet (New Valve Technology); some clinical sources call the associated company MEDIRA, so ownership/legal-name history remains a gap rather than grounds for a silent rename.
