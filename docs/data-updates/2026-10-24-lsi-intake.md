## Review resolutions and limits

- C-MIC II, III and IV retain separate configuration evidence. Cardiovalve historical mitral research is distinct from the tricuspid program. Historical Xeltis and Heart Biotech configurations are not pooled with later platforms.
- HeartFocus v1.1.1 and v1.3.0 retain exact FDA submission scopes and compatibility; cardiac-image acquisition guidance is separate from automated disease diagnosis. Medividia is a planning/research workflow.
- Publication review preserved the existing CorWave/EASE-HF/EASE-HF2 endpoint timeframes and added source-located assessment periods to 17 new-trial endpoint summaries. These are registered designs, not observed results.
- Four News entries resolve company/program associations admitted in the same batch, matching the approved local preview.
- Missing exact versions, materials, results, authorization records and patent connections remain explicit gaps. Search results and source-access refusals do not establish absence. Literature query caps and incomplete non-US registry coverage limit completeness.
- New configurations use explicit illustration holds. No new device artwork or unverified reuse rights were approved. No raw evidence, licensed full text, private watchlists or contact data are included in this repository.
