2026-09-11

CLOSURE-AF sex-specific analysis raises new caution about LAAO in older women who can tolerate DOAC therapy

A sex-specific analysis of the randomized CLOSURE-AF trial found that worse outcomes with LAAO versus best medical therapy were concentrated in women. Among women, the primary composite endpoint occurred in 58.7% with LAAO versus 35.1% with medical therapy, while no comparable adverse treatment effect was seen in men; the strongest interaction involved cardiovascular or unexplained death.

Source: TCTMD / ESC 2026 analysis

2026-09-11

CMS finalizes expanded Medicare coverage for TAVR, including asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis

CMS finalized a major update to the U.S. National Coverage Determination for TAVR. Medicare coverage now extends to selected patients with asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis under coverage-with-evidence-development, while symptomatic severe AS no longer carries that CED requirement. CMS also preserved the multidisciplinary heart-team model while allowing more flexibility in chart-review triage, operator configuration, and volume criteria.

Source: TCTMD / CMS

2026-09-10

Emboline receives FDA clearance for the Emboliner embolic-protection system for TAVR

The FDA granted 510(k) clearance to Emboline’s Emboliner Embolic Protection System for use during TAVR. The self-expanding filter is designed to protect all three cerebral vessels while also capturing embolic material that could travel to systemic organs, giving it a broader protection concept than cerebral-only filters.

Source: Emboline / Cardiac Interventions Today

2026-09-06

India launches national Valve Registry for transcatheter valve interventions

India launched the India Valve Registry, a collaborative multicenter initiative designed to collect de-identified real-world data on patients undergoing transcatheter valve interventions and their long-term outcomes. The registry aims to generate India-specific evidence rather than relying primarily on European and North American datasets, and participating centers will contribute standardized treatment and outcome information.

Source: India Valves 2026 / DD India

Heart Failure

2026-09-09

Large U.S. study highlights persistent heart-failure risk and underuse of guideline-directed therapy after TAVR

An analysis of more than 37,000 U.S. TAVR patients found that heart failure remains common before and after valve replacement and that evidence-based HF medications are frequently underused. Within one year of TAVR, 18.6% experienced an acute HF episode; HF hospitalization and all-cause mortality reached 8.8% and 9.8% at one year, rising to 13.8% and 17.9% at two years.

Source: Cardiovascular Business / Journal of Cardiovascular Medicine

2026-09-09

Tempus receives ARPA-H award to test an autonomous AI agent in heart failure

Tempus said an award of up to $9.5 million will support development and prospective multicenter validation of an AI agent intended to coordinate heart-failure care; the system remains investigational.

Source: Tempus AI

2026-09-12

AI-powered ultrasound robotic system guides transcatheter closure of congenital heart disease in China

A Chinese team reported performing a transcatheter closure of an atrial septal defect in a 48-year-old patient using an AI-powered ultrasound robotic system. Public reports describe the platform as combining automated ultrasound acquisition, lesion identification, 3D anatomical reconstruction, path planning, and real-time procedural navigation, while clinicians retained responsibility for the intervention and device deployment.

Source: Sixth Medical Center of the Chinese PLA General Hospital

2026-09-09

Carvolix submits TAVIPILOT Software for CE marking, targeting European launch in early 2027

Carvolix announced that it submitted the European regulatory dossier for CE marking of TAVIPILOT Software, its AI-based intraoperative guidance system for TAVR. The software has already received FDA clearance in the United States, and Carvolix now expects a possible CE Mark by year-end 2026 followed by an initial commercial rollout in France and Germany in early 2027.

Source: Carvolix
