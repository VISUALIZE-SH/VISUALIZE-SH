This site does not track you

VISUALIZE·SH does not use analytics, advertising pixels, browser fingerprinting, tracking cookies, or tracking storage. The site stores one localStorage value to remember the selected light, dark, or system color theme; it is not used for analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking. Because the site does not set cookies, it does not show a cookie-consent banner.

The app loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Those requests are governed by Google’s privacy terms; VISUALIZE·SH does not receive or use data from them. The static-site hosting provider may process ordinary network request logs to deliver and protect the service.

Newsletter signup

Selecting a newsletter signup control opens a separately hosted Zoho Campaigns form. Information you enter there goes directly to Zoho and is governed by the privacy and consent information presented on that form. Subscriber addresses, contact lists, and recipient exports are not stored in this public repository or in public newsletter pages.

Public content and source links

Newsletter issues and atlas data are public. Source links open third-party sites, whose own privacy terms apply. No subscriber-specific identifiers are added to those links by VISUALIZE·SH.
